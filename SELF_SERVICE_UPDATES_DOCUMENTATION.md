## <span style="color: #2d3748;">WHAT ARE SELF-SERVICE UPDATES?</span>

<span style="color: #4a5568;">Self-Service Updates are like having a personal assistant for your policy details. Instead of calling customer service or visiting a branch, you can update your personal information directly through the MediCare Companion system whenever it's convenient for you.</span>

## <span style="color: #2d3748;">WHY BENEFICIARIES CANNOT USE IT</span>

<div style="background-color: #fff5f5; border: 2px solid #f56565; padding: 20px; border-radius: 10px; margin: 20px 0;">

### <span style="color: #c53030;">SECURITY AND LEGAL REASONS</span>

<span style="color: #4a5568;">Beneficiaries are blocked from self-service updates for important reasons:</span>

#### <span style="color: #e53e3e;">Legal Authority</span>
<span style="color: #4a5568;">Only policyholders have the legal right to modify policy-related information</span>

#### <span style="color: #e53e3e;">Financial Protection</span>
<span style="color: #4a5568;">Prevents changes to banking details or contact information that could affect the policy</span>

#### <span style="color: #e53e3e;">Identity Security</span>
<span style="color: #4a5568;">Protects against unauthorized changes by people who shouldn't have access</span>

#### <span style="color: #e53e3e;">Policy Integrity</span>
<span style="color: #4a5568;">Ensures only the policyholder can make changes that affect their contractual relationship</span>

</div>

## <span style="color: #2d3748;">DECEASED FLAG RESTRICTION</span>

<div style="background-color: #f7fafc; border: 2px solid #718096; padding: 20px; border-radius: 10px; margin: 20px 0;">

### <span style="color: #2d3748;">WHEN SOMEONE PASSES AWAY</span>

<span style="color: #4a5568;">Even policyholders cannot use self-service updates when the deceased flag is active:</span>

<div style="background-color: rgba(113, 128, 150, 0.1); padding: 15px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 15px 0;">

- <span style="color: #4a5568;">**All Updates Frozen**</span> - No self-service changes are allowed during claims processing
- <span style="color: #4a5568;">**Claims Focus**</span> - The system focuses entirely on claims support, not policy modifications
- <span style="color: #4a5568;">**Protection**</span> - Prevents accidental changes when the policy is in a sensitive state
- <span style="color: #4a5568;">**Guidance Provided**</span> - Users are directed to contact MediCare for assistance with any needed changes

</div>

</div>
