## <span style="color: #2d3748;">WHAT IS THE WHAT-IF CALCULATOR?</span>

<span style="color: #4a5568;">The What-If Premium Calculator is like a crystal ball for your insurance policy. It lets you see how changing your coverage amount would affect your premium payments before you make any actual changes. Think of it as a "try before you buy" feature for your policy adjustments.</span>

## <span style="color: #2d3748;">WHY BENEFICIARIES CANNOT USE IT</span>

<div style="background-color: #fff5f5; border: 2px solid #f56565; padding: 20px; border-radius: 10px; margin: 20px 0;">

### <span style="color: #c53030;">SECURITY AND LEGAL REASONS</span>

<span style="color: #4a5568;">Beneficiaries are blocked from using the What-If Calculator for important reasons:</span>

#### <span style="color: #e53e3e;">Financial Authority</span>
<span style="color: #4a5568;">Only policyholders have the legal authority to make financial decisions about the policy</span>

#### <span style="color: #e53e3e;">Contractual Rights</span>
<span style="color: #4a5568;">Insurance contracts give only policyholders the right to modify coverage and premiums</span>

#### <span style="color: #e53e3e;">Fraud Prevention</span>
<span style="color: #4a5568;">Prevents unauthorized parties from experimenting with policy changes that could affect the policyholder</span>

#### <span style="color: #e53e3e;">Claims Focus</span>
<span style="color: #4a5568;">When beneficiaries need access, it's typically for claims, not policy modifications</span>

</div>

## <span style="color: #2d3748;">DECEASED FLAG RESTRICTION</span>

<div style="background-color: #f7fafc; border: 2px solid #718096; padding: 20pxpx; border-radius: 10px; margin: 20px 0;">

### <span style="color: #2d3748;">WHEN SOMEONE PASSES AWAY</span>

<span style="color: #4a5568;">Even policyholders cannot use the What-If Calculator when the deceased flag is active:</span>

<div style="background-color: rgba(113, 128, 150, 0.1); padding: 15px; border-radius: 8px; margin: 15px 0;">

- <span style="color: #4a5568;">**Feature Frozen**</span> - All self-service features are suspended during claims processing
- <span style="color: #4a5568;">**Claims Focus**</span> - The system focuses entirely on claims support, not policy changes
- <span style="color: #4a5568;">**Protection**</span> - Prevents accidental changes when the policy is in a sensitive state
- <span style="color: #4a5568;">**Guidance Provided**</span> - Users are directed to contact MediCare for claims-related assistance

</div>
